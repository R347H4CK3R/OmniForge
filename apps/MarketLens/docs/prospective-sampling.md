# Next research increment: unbiased observation log
Not implemented in first preview. Local paper entries are user-selected and cannot establish edge.

Collect at most one immutable forecast per observed market, at open+5 minutes (strict +/-5-second eligibility; missed windows are explicitly missed, never retrospectively backfilled). Record all eligible observed windows irrespective of probability, not only manually selected paper positions. Inputs: exact market rules/target and receive times, original independent candles with availability cutoff, quote/depth/receive time, fees and fixed model version. Forecast expires separately from immutable history. Query official outcomes by exact ticker and retain outcome revisions/audit trail. Foreground-only app cannot provide complete 24/7 sample; report coverage and selection limitation. A separately authorized persistent server collector is necessary for continuous coverage.

Freeze protocol/model before prospective period. Chronological train/calibration/holdout split grouped by market, embargo >= horizon, all fitting only on past data. Compare paired Brier/logloss/reliability against market midpoint baseline and after-cost execution simulation using asks, observed depth and latency, never midpoint fills. Bootstrap by day and report confidence bounds; do not call a probability calibrated or favorable until independent out-of-sample net-cost evidence supports it. No authenticated trading or real orders are necessary or included.
